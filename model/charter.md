# EchoBraid charter

Purpose: a neurodivergent-affirming, ASD-aligned learning companion that keeps
the learner's pace, asks before it senses, and offers a pause before anything
else when things run high, with every rule it follows proved in Lean.

Stakeholders:
- Learner: an autistic or otherwise neurodivergent person using EchoBraid to learn, write and regulate.
- Educator: a teacher, therapist or parent who recommends EchoBraid and needs to trust what it will and will not do.
- Engine maintainer: the person who reads the engine's frontier and keeps its pipeline running.

Concerns:
- Pace: the learner sets the pace; the companion never pushes, scores or maximises engagement.
- Consent: no sensor (microphone, voice, reminders) turns on without the learner's own grant, and one action turns everything off.
- Regulation: when semantic drift is divergent the first and only offer is a pause, and every breathing rhythm is safe.
- Retention: a reflection kept for a day is gone after a day, and the learner can clear it at any time.
- Trust: every decision is made by a guest whose rules are proved, and anyone can check the holo before it runs.

Features:
- Soft loop: a writing loop whose drift, coherence and pause offer are proved bounded and ordered.
- Breathe: the 4-7-8 rhythm and every rhythm the engine proves safe, counted down tick by tick.
- Memory: a prime-length memory game whose tiles are drawn by Rule 30 and whose verdicts are proved fair.
- Reflect: prime-numbered prompts and a private journal the learner can seal with their Prism ID.
- Logic: a workbench for quorum intersection and ring-buffer invariants, with the counterexample to the naive quorum claim.
- Care: consent and retention, decided by the guest.
- Engine: an autonomous pipeline that discovers, proves and ships new rhythms, one per tick.
